Find every piece of personal or sensitive data in the document below. Each line starts with its number ("L012| "). Only point at the data: masking (one "*" per word) is done afterwards by code, so never rewrite the document.
</task>

<categories>
NAME: names of people (patient, relatives, contacts).
ID: identification numbers (NIF, Cartão de Cidadão, NISS, nº de utente, passport, insurance policy, biometric IDs).
CONTACT: phone numbers, e-mails, personal addresses (street, number, floor, postal code, city).

FINANCIAL: card number, expiry date, CVV, IBAN, income, insurance details.
HEALTH: conditions, symptoms, diagnoses, medication, test results, measurements (height, weight, blood pressure, blood type), family medical history.
SPECIAL: ethnic origin, religion, political opinion, sexual orientation, genetic data.
PRIVATE_LIFE: marital status, children, habits (smoking, alcohol), addictions.
AGE: a person's age.
OCCUPATION: a person's job and employer.
CLINICIAN: the health professional's name and professional registration number.
SEX: a person's sex or gender.
</categories>

<keep>
Never flag: document titles, section headers and field labels ("Nome:", "Telefone:"); the document's own date and reference codes; names of institutions, hospitals and departments; generic vocabulary that does not describe this person.
</keep>

<method>
Read the whole document first. Then go line by line and decide, from the context (labels, the surrounding sentence) and not only from the format, whether each value identifies or describes a specific person. When unsure, flag it.
</method>

<rules>
- "text" is copied exactly from its line: same accents, case, spacing and inner punctuation.
- Return the complete value (full name, full address, full date such as "12 de maio de 1980"), without its label or trailing punctuation.
- Leave out titles (Dr., Dra., Sr.) and unit words that say nothing alone ("anos", "kg").
- One entry per occurrence: a value repeated on two lines is listed twice.
- A value that continues on the next line is returned as one entry per line, each with the part of the text that is on that line.
- "reason": at most 8 words on why it is sensitive.
</rules>
